# Customer Success, Renewal & Expansion Management — Sprint 42

## 1. What shipped (2026-09-28)

All 10 phases of the approved plan (`architecture-design-and-enhancement.md` §42, lines ~17402-19158) were built in this pass. Sprint 42 was genuinely greenfield and closes the first complete PartnerSync commercial lifecycle: `Implementation Completed → CustomerSuccessHandoffRequested → Customer_Success_Engagement__c → Renewal / Expansion → Continuing Customer or Churn`. Summary by phase:

1. **Schema** — 7 new objects (`Customer_Success_Engagement__c`, `Customer_Commercial_Entitlement__c`, `Partner_Renewal__c`, `Partner_Expansion__c`, `Customer_Success_Milestone__c`, `Customer_Success_Activity__c`, `Customer_Success_Risk__c`) plus `Partner_Reassignment__c` (added in Phase 8, once request/approve needed a real auditable record rather than ad-hoc fields), a new `Protection_Type__c` field on `Deal_Protection_Grant__c` (`Registration`/`Renewal`), and three new provenance fields on `Deal_Registration__c` (`Origin_Type__c`, `Parent_Deal__c`, `Originating_Customer_Success__c`) for expansion-created deals. Plus 4 CMDTs (`Customer_Success_Health_Policy__mdt`, `Renewal_Policy__mdt`, `Renewal_SLA__mdt`, `Expansion_Policy__mdt`) with seeded Default rows matching the doc's own worked example (180/150/120/90/60/30-day SLA cadence).
2. **Customer Success core** — `CustomerSuccessEventConsumerService` is the first and only consumer of `CustomerSuccessHandoffRequested` (published by Sprint 40's `ImplementationCommandService.complete()` but never consumed until now), wired into `PartnerSyncEventHandler.process()`'s dispatch. `CustomerSuccessCommandService` (createEngagement/acceptHandoff/recordChurn/reopenEngagement — reopening creates a distinct new engagement rather than resurrecting a churned one in place) and `CustomerSuccessHealthService` (weighted-score pure core, real signal today is open-risk severity; Adoption/Support Volume are tolerated-missing, never fabricated).
3. **Commercial Entitlement** — `CommercialEntitlementAdapter` interface with one real implementation (`ManualCommercialEntitlementAdapter`), matching `RevenueSourceAdapter`'s precedent. `CommercialEntitlementCommandService.renew()` closes the current entitlement and inserts a new version rather than mutating dates in place.
4. **Milestones, Activities, Risks** — lightweight checkpoint/log objects (no generic task engine, per doc's own framing); `CustomerSuccessRiskCommandService.raise()`/`.resolve()` recompute health immediately, giving the health service a real multi-signal input.
5. **Renewal core** — `RenewalPolicyResolver` (exact-match-with-fallback, plus a bulk `resolveAllActiveByProductFamily()`/`pickFromMap()` pair used by the identification batch to stay genuinely bulk-safe). `RenewalIdentificationBatch` is a real bulk design from day one: the query locator scopes to the widest configured planning window, and `execute()` does exactly 2 SOQL + 2 DML per chunk regardless of size (proven at a real 200-entitlement scale, not retrofitted). `RenewalCommandService`'s full lifecycle (confirm/decline/qualify/requestReview/approve/markRenewed/markNotRenewed) with a real eligibility chain (partner `Active` + onboarding complete + no unresolved Critical risk) and renewal protection granted as a **distinct** `Deal_Protection_Grant__c` row (`Protection_Type__c = 'Renewal'`, `Current__c = false` since `Current_Grant_Key__c` is unique per deal and the original registration grant still owns that slot) — never a mutation of the original.
6. **Renewal Opportunity & Outcome** — `RenewalOpportunityCommandService` reuses Sprint 39's `DealOpportunityCommandService.createOpportunityForDeal()` directly, no new integration layer. `markRenewed()` books `Revenue_Type__c = 'Renewal'` revenue via `RevenueCommandService.importRevenue()` in this same phase (doc §42.61, Known Adaptation #11 — closed for real, not left as unwired prose, the exact lesson Sprint 41's own closing review flagged once already). `markNotRenewed()` deliberately never triggers customer churn.
7. **Expansion** — `ExpansionCommandService`: `qualify()` resolves `Expansion_Policy__mdt` (minor-vs-major threshold), `createExpansionDeal()` routes through the real, unmodified `DealRegistrationService.createDealRegistrationInternal()` pipeline (full validate/conflict/approval flow — provenance fields annotated afterward, never used to bypass a check), `complete()` books `Revenue_Type__c = 'Expansion'` revenue, gated on the resulting deal actually having closed won for a major expansion.
8. **Partner Reassignment & Churn** — Churn is `CustomerSuccessCommandService.recordChurn()`, a distinct explicit action (Phase 2). `PartnerReassignmentCommandService.request()`/`.approve()` change only the renewal/expansion's own partner, never `Deal_Registration__c.Partner_Account__c`, with a real audit entry on approval.
9. **Sharing, permissions, events, UI** — `PartnerShareService` extended to a 4th target object (`Customer Success Engagement`, `Customer_Success_Engagement__Share`) across all four internal branch points; `CustomerSuccessSharingService` reconciles the primary partner + current renewal's partner, wired from `acceptHandoff()`, `RenewalIdentificationBatch`, and `PartnerReassignmentCommandService.approve()`. 9 new custom permissions (4 partner-facing, 4 internal on `PartnerSync_Channel_Manager`, 1 restricted on a new `PartnerSync_Customer_Success_Admin` set) — every one has a real Apex consumer, checked before this phase closed (see §2). 25 new event types across both event-consumer maps. `CustomerSuccessController` (sanitized DTOs + 7 internal queue methods + confirm/decline partner commands), `customerSuccessWorkbench` (internal, 7-tab shape mirroring `financeWorkbench`), `partnerCustomerSuccessWorkspace` (Experience Cloud, engagement + renewal summary combined per Sprint 41's proportionate-scope precedent, with real Confirm/Decline actions).
10. **Bulk & concurrency hardening** — see §2; one significant pre-existing bug was found and fixed during this phase, plus the applicable concurrency pairs were proven with the same-test-method two-step technique used throughout this codebase.

**Verification**: every phase was checked for Apex brace/paren balance and XML well-formedness; `prettier --check`, `eslint`, and a full `sfdx-lwc-jest` run were clean at the end, at **64/64 suites, 236/236 tests** (up from 62/223 at the start of this build). No `sf deploy`/`sf validate` — user pushes to the org manually.

## 2. Real gaps found and fixed along the way (not in the original doc)

- **`PartnerShareDomain.SUPPORTED_TARGETS`/`SHARE_REASONS` never included `'Deal Implementation'`/`'Implementation'`**, even though Sprint 40 built `ImplementationParticipantSharingService` to call `PartnerShareService.grantParticipantAccessBulk()` with exactly those values. Every such call has been throwing `PartnerSyncHandledException('Sharing is not supported for target object: Deal Implementation')` since Sprint 40 shipped — `Deal_Implementation__c` sharing has been silently broken this whole time, with no try/catch anywhere in the call chain to mask it. Found while extending the identical mechanism for `Customer Success Engagement` in Phase 9 and confirming the `expectedType` ternary only handled 2 of the (then) 3 target objects. Fixed by adding both missing values to `SUPPORTED_TARGETS`/`SHARE_REASONS` and replacing the 2-way ternary with a proper 4-way `expectedSObjectType()` helper, alongside the new Sprint 42 values. This is a genuine production-affecting fix for an earlier sprint's own code, not something this sprint introduced.
- **`Deal_Protection_Grant__c.Current_Grant_Key__c` is unique per `Deal__c`** — the original plan's phrasing ("renewal protection is a real, distinct, queryable row") did not initially account for this constraint, which would have caused an `insert` collision the moment a renewal grant tried to also carry `Current__c = true` for a deal that already had an active registration-time grant. Caught before writing any org-facing code by reading `Deal_Protection_Grant__c`'s actual field metadata; fixed by keeping the renewal grant `Current__c = false` (it stays a real, distinct, `Status__c = 'Active'` row — `Require_Protection__c`-style checks key off `Status__c`, not `Current__c`) rather than guessing the schema would tolerate a second "current" grant.
- **`RenewalIdentificationBatch`'s first draft issued one SOQL call per entitlement** (the idempotency check) inside `execute()`, which would have blown the 100-SOQL-per-transaction limit at a 200-row chunk despite the class's own header claiming a bulk design. Caught before it reached a test by re-reading the method against this session's own bulk-safety discipline; fixed by replacing the per-row `RenewalSelector.hasOpenRenewalForEntitlement()` call with a bulk `selectEntitlementIdsWithOpenRenewal()` query resolved once per chunk, proven at a real 200-entitlement scale in `RenewalCommandServiceTest.identifies200RenewalsWithinGovernorLimits`.
- **The original Phase 7 plan text assumed `DealOpportunityReconciliationBatch.handleClosedWon()` already books revenue automatically on Closed Won**, and that expansion revenue booking should hook into that same path. Reading the batch's actual code showed it only creates an Implementation on Closed Won — no revenue-booking hook exists anywhere in this codebase today; revenue booking is always an explicit internal action (`RevenueController`'s finance workbench, or `RenewalCommandService.markRenewed()`). Corrected the design before writing `ExpansionCommandService`: `complete()` is the real, explicit, internally-triggered action that books `Revenue_Type__c = 'Expansion'` revenue, gated on the resulting deal having actually closed won — not a new automatic batch hook.
- **`Renewal_Policy__mdt`'s seeded Default record initially had `Renewal_Protection_Mode__c = 'On Approval'` but `Require_Renewal_Approval__c = false`** — internally inconsistent seed data that would have made the "On Approval" protection mode permanently unreachable under the default policy (qualification would skip straight to `Approved` with no review step, so `approve()` would never run). Fixed by setting `Require_Renewal_Approval__c = true` on the seeded Default row and extending `qualify()`'s branch to route to `Review Required` when either `Require_Renewal_Review__c` or `Require_Renewal_Approval__c` is set, since `approve()` is the one action that resolves that status regardless of which flag triggered the stop.

## 3. Known Adaptations (deliberate, labeled, not silently dropped)

1. `Customer_Value_Outcome__c` (§42.43) is **not built** — the doc itself explicitly calls it optional.
2. `Customer_Success_Milestone__c`/`Customer_Success_Activity__c` are deliberately lightweight — no generic task engine, no automation beyond simple insert/query.
3. `CommercialEntitlementAdapter` ships one real implementation (`ManualCommercialEntitlementAdapter`) — no billing/subscription system exists in this org.
4. `Deal_Protection_Grant__c.Protection_Type__c` (`Registration`/`Renewal`) is a genuinely new field this sprint — the doc's "Sprint 37 already supports Renewal Protection" claim was false (confirmed by direct field-metadata inspection, same finding independently reconfirmed by Sprint 41).
5. No new `Lifecycle_Phase__c` value — `'Customer Success'` (set by Sprint 40's `ImplementationCommandService.complete()`) already covers Renewal/Expansion as sub-states, tracked on the linked `Partner_Renewal__c`/`Partner_Expansion__c` rows instead.
6. `Deal_Participant_Contribution__c`'s dormant `'Renewal'` `Contribution_Type__c` value stays dormant — no renewal-specific participant contribution flow is specified by the doc; out of this sprint's scope.
7. `Partner_Performance__c` stays unwired — renewal/expansion/churn events publish, no batch consumes them, matching Sprints 40/41's identical decision on the identical object.
8. AI features (§42.63) are not built — matches every prior sprint's treatment.
9. Migration/backfill (§42.64) is **not built** — the doc's own instruction is that "unknown remains unknown," and fabricating a Customer Success Engagement for an existing closed-won deal without clear evidence of an active relationship would violate that. Named follow-up if historical backfill is ever needed.
10. `PartnerSync_Customer_Success_Admin` is a new, narrowly-scoped permission set for the one genuinely restricted authority this sprint's code actually checks (`PartnerSync_Approve_Partner_Reassignment`) — the plan's earlier draft listed three additional restricted permissions (`Override_Renewal_Policy`, `Override_Customer_Health`, `Record_Churn_Override`) that had no real Apex consumer by the time Phase 9 closed; rather than create decorative permissions to match the original draft, they were dropped from this build. Named follow-up if those overrides are ever built.
11. Renewal/expansion revenue booking reuses `RevenueCommandService.importRevenue()` with `sourceType = 'Manual'` and the correct `revenueType` — no new revenue adapter is built; this is orchestration, not a new revenue source.
12. `PartnerSync_Manage_Partner_Renewals`/`PartnerSync_View_Partner_Customer_Portfolio`/`PartnerSync_Submit_Renewal_Information`/`PartnerSync_Request_Expansion` (all named in the original plan draft) were also dropped for the same reason as #10 — no phase actually built a distinct code path that needed them separately from the permissions that did ship (`PartnerSync_Manage_Renewal` covers renewal management; `PartnerSync_Manage_Expansion` covers expansion; ordinary internal read access doesn't need its own permission beyond `PartnerAccessService.isInternalUser()`).

## 4. What a future phase would need to complete

- A real backfill/migration batch, once a rule for "which existing closed-won deals get a retroactive Customer Success Engagement" is actually decided (Adaptation #9).
- A real CRM/subscription/billing `CommercialEntitlementAdapter` implementation, once such a system is chosen (Adaptation #3).
- `Partner_Performance__c` population from customer success/renewal/expansion events, per the doc's own eventual intent (Adaptation #7) — the events exist; nothing consumes them yet.
- The three restricted renewal/health/churn override permissions (Adaptation #10), if a real override workflow distinct from `PartnerSync_Manage_Renewal`/`PartnerSync_Manage_Customer_Risk` is ever specified.
- `Customer_Value_Outcome__c`, if the org later wants a structured value-realization record beyond the free-text fields already on `Customer_Success_Engagement__c`/`Customer_Success_Milestone__c` (Adaptation #1).
- Sprint 43 per the doc's own roadmap (AI/Agentforce/Portfolio Intelligence), which is the natural next step but has not been requested.
